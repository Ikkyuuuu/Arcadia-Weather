    Automatic mode

    How scenes change

    Automatic follows your local time, then checks the weather.

    1. 01
      Start with the time

      Morning & afternoon → Day 1
      Evening → Day 4
      Night → Day 3

      Sample hours: day 06:00–17:59, evening 18:00–20:59, night 21:00–05:59.
    2. 02
      Let the clouds roll in

      Cloudy weather → Day 2 during daytime or evening. Night stays Day 3.

    3. 03
      Rain takes priority

      Rain, drizzle, showers, or thunderstorms → Day 5, even at night.

    Weather refreshes every five minutes. A manually selected scene stays until you enable Automatic again.
